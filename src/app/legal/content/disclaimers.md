## What a Milo does and doesn't do

A Milo is a preconfigured AI workflow. It runs in your own ChatGPT account and saves its results to a spreadsheet in your own Google account. On a schedule, it researches publicly available business information. It does not contact anyone, and it does not guarantee leads, sales or any other business result.

## AI can be wrong

Milos use AI to find and summarize information from public websites. Both the AI and the source websites can be incomplete, outdated or wrong. A Milo may occasionally:

- list a business that doesn't fit;
- misread a detail; or
- repeat an entry.

Check anything important before you rely on it.

## What “qualified” means

A qualified business is one that appears to match the criteria on the product page, based on public information at the time of research. It does not confirm a business's licensing, insurance, financial health, reputation or interest in buying from you. If a license matters to you, verify it with the state licensing agency.

## Daily targets are maximums

“Up to five per scheduled workday, when available” is a ceiling, not a promise. Some days the Milo may find fewer businesses, or none. This is more likely in smaller markets, and as the pool of new businesses gets used up.

Milos depend on ChatGPT and Google, which can change their features, limits, permissions and prices without notice to us. If we can't restore it, you may request a prorated refund under our Terms of Service.
